## Search and citations

Knowledge search can find workspace material and standards clauses together. Check source processing status and search scope, then add product material to broaden the evidence available for review.

Include the standard, edition, and clause number in citations, and check that the clause supports the claim. Ask the agent to write from clauses confirmed by search and separate requirements that need further verification.

## Review documents

- Distinguish normative clauses from informative material and recommendations.
- Separate confirmed product facts from items an owner needs to decide.
- Collect sections needing more evidence with a label such as `[GAP]`.
- List cited standards, editions, and clauses at the end to simplify review.

Use original standards within their license terms. Compare the edition, clause, and content against your licensed copy to check both accuracy and permitted use.

## Write medical device documents

Specify code, product information, and standards evidence together when drafting requirements, architecture, risk management, or SOUP documents. Manage skills in [Tools and skills](/en/guides/plugins-skills) and save results to documents or canvases.

Owners and experts review the generated draft to prepare it for submission. Safety classification and certification conformity are determined through the applicable assessment process using product evidence. See [Medical device and certification documents](/en/guides/medical-device-docs).
