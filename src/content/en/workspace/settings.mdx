Open **Settings** from the account menu to manage your account, workspace, tools, and usage. Available sections depend on your current workspace and permissions.

## Settings sections

| Group | Section | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | General · Notifications | Profile, theme, language, and notification preferences |
| Workspace | General · Members | Name, description, URL, default AI model, and team members |
| AI Agent | Tools & skills | Skills, connected external services, and editor MCP connections |
| Plans & Billing | Plan · Usage | Current plan, billing, and AI usage |
| Manage | Archive | Find, restore, or delete archived chats and inbox entries |

Open Members in a team workspace to manage teammates.

## Workspace general settings

Edit the name and description and manage the workspace URL. **Default AI Model** is the workspace default for automations. Distinguish it from the model selected in chat. See [Model overview](/en/models/overview).

Deleting a workspace and transferring ownership require appropriate permissions. An ownership transfer completes when the recipient accepts the request.

## Tools and skills

Manage skill instructions, external services used by AI, and access from external editors. External service connections and MCP editor connections serve different purposes. MCP lets external agents search and read Specify material.

See [Tools and skills](/en/guides/plugins-skills) and [Editor connections](/en/mcp/connect).

## Usage and archive

Usage belongs to the current workspace. See [Usage and AI budget](/en/models/usage) for how to check it.

The archive can restore chats and inbox entries. Deleting an inbox entry keeps its generated documents and execution records. This is separate from document trash.
