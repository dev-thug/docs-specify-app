Teammates join a workspace by accepting an invitation with their own accounts.

## Roles

| Role | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Holds workspace ownership and management authority. |
| Admin | Manages members and workspace settings. |
| Editor | Edits permitted documents and canvases. |
| Viewer | Reads permitted material. |

## Invite and manage

Open **Settings** → **Workspace** → **Members** and select **Invite Member**. Enter the email address and role, then choose **Send Invitation**. The invitation must be accepted using the account with the invited email address.

Use the member list to change roles, suspend, or remove members within your permissions. Pending invitations can be resent or canceled.

## Team and personal workspaces

To invite teammates and manage roles, open Members in a team workspace. Use personal workspaces for individual work. See the [pricing page](https://specify.app/pricing) for plan terms and limits.

## External agent access

Each member uses their own access authorization for external editor connections. For MCP writes, check both an Editor-or-higher role and the token’s write scope. See [Tokens and access policies](/en/mcp/access).
