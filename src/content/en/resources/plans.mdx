Specify offers personal and team plans. Choose one based on how much developer documentation you write and maintain and on your AI workload.

<Note>
  The [pricing page](https://specify.app/en/pricing) is the source of truth for prices, usage limits, and billing terms. This page is a summary to help you understand how the plans are structured. Check the pricing page for amounts.
</Note>

## Plan overview

| Plan | Best for | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Getting started organizing personal context | Basic AI usage, unlimited documents, 100MB storage, collaboration tool integrations |
| **Pro** | Individuals with recurring work | More AI usage than the Free plan, 1GB storage, GPT and Claude models, daily briefing and AI agent teammate |
| **Max** | Individuals with heavier AI workloads | 5× the AI usage of Pro, 10GB storage |
| **Team** | Team collaboration | More generous per-seat AI usage than Pro (shared workspace pool), 2GB per seat, role-based permissions, shared team-level background delegation |
| **Enterprise** | Large organizations | Unlimited AI usage, SSO, audit log, custom storage |

## AI features by plan

| Feature | Free | Pro · Max · Team · Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Available models | Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna | All models (including Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-5.6 Terra) |
| Reasoning effort | Fixed at Off | Choose from Off to Max |
| Document projects (GitHub-based document generation and automatic updates) | Not available | Available |
| [Workspace RAG](/en/knowledge/rag) (document and file search) | Available | Available |

<Note>
  Workspace RAG works within your plan's storage limit. If uploaded files exceed the limit, new files aren't indexed.
</Note>

For differences between models, see [Models overview](/en/models/overview) and [Choosing a model](/en/models/choosing).

## Free trial and billing cycle

- A **14-day free trial** is available on the Pro and Team plans. On the Max plan, paid features start right after payment.
- Choose **annual billing** to get 2 months free compared with monthly billing.
- You can start on the Free plan without a credit card.

## Change plans

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time, and changes take effect immediately.

## Roles on the Team plan

The Team plan supports four roles (Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer), and you can add up to 100 members per workspace. See [Invite your team and manage roles](/en/workspace/members).
