Choose a Specify plan based on usage and collaboration needs. See the [pricing page](https://specify.app/pricing) for current prices, storage, AI limits, trials, and billing terms.

## Check your plan

Open **Settings** → **Plans & Billing** → **Plan** to check the current workspace's plan and billing details. Personal and team workspace subscriptions are separate.

## AI models and usage

The Free plan supports Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna. Other supported models require Pro or above. See [Model overview](/en/models/overview) for reasoning effort support and plan restrictions.

Check usage for the current workspace. See [Usage and AI budget](/en/models/usage).
