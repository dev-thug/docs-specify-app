Use this workflow to turn release notes and the current FAQ into a customer announcement and a focused FAQ update. It helps PM, support, and product marketing teams keep feature scope and usage limits consistent.

## What you get

Create a change summary, customer announcement draft, and FAQ revisions for PM, support, and product marketing. The screens show actual output from fictional <strong>RelayDesk Demo</strong> materials, not a message sent to real customers.

## Prepare the materials

<a href="/examples/en/relaydesk-demo.zip" download="relaydesk-demo.zip">Download the example materials</a> and upload these sources to your workspace.

| Material | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| `relaydesk-release-brief.md` | Version 1.4 scope and claims to avoid |
| `relaydesk-faq-before.md` | Existing version 1.3 customer explanations |
| `relaydesk-security-current.md` | Current role restrictions and SSO eligibility |

Use approved change records and product descriptions in real work. Planned functionality, internal implementation completion, and availability to customers are different states.

## Follow the workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare both new information and existing guidance">Include the FAQ to update, not just the new change record. Clearly identify versions and audiences.</Step>
  <Step title="Define audiences and outputs in a new chat">Use <strong>New Chat</strong> to request specific outputs, such as a PM summary, customer notice, and support FAQ.</Step>
  <Step title="Separate changed explanations from retained limits">Ask for limits, eligible roles, and unavailable features alongside changes. Do not invent menu paths or performance figures absent from the sources.</Step>
  <Step title="Review saved drafts before distribution">Save the output as a document and verify consistent limits, roles, and scope across all three sections. Publishing and sending remain separate actions for the owner.</Step>
</Steps>

```text
Read RelayDesk Demo's 1.4 change brief, existing FAQ 1.3, and current security and operations policy.
Write three sections: PM change summary / customer announcement draft / FAQ revisions.
Explain CSV export benefits together with row limits and role restrictions.
Distinguish FAQ answers that change from answers that stay the same. Include source IDs.
Do not invent menu paths, release dates, performance figures, or unavailable features.
Save 'Customer update and FAQ drafts'. Do not edit the original FAQ or send anything externally.
```
