Give customers consistent information by preparing release announcements and FAQs from the same evidence. Confirm current behavior and conditions, then prepare the drafts for publication.

## Prepare the sources

Prepare approved release notes, the existing FAQ, and current usage policies. Distinguish implemented code, customer availability, and future plans. Record the reference date and applicable version for each source.

## Review announcements and FAQs together

Ask in chat to separate what customers can now do, answers that change, and limits and conditions that remain. Draft a change summary, customer announcement, and FAQ updates, then check the evidence for each statement. For complex features, review internal feature documents and flow diagrams to find misleading explanations.

## Maintain them through later changes

Internal documents linked to GitHub can be updated directly after pushes. An owner reviews availability, permissions, limitations, and effective dates in those results to prepare customer communications. After review, publish or send them through your customer channel.

## Example chat request

```text
Read the current release notes, existing FAQ, and usage policies.
Draft a change summary, customer announcement, and FAQ updates from the same evidence.
Separate changed explanations from limits that remain, with reference dates and source documents.
Do not invent schedules, performance figures, or menu paths absent from the sources.
```

<Note>When sources conflict, check applicability and approval status rather than choosing solely by date. If behavior itself needs to change, create an improvement document for the coding agent to read over MCP.</Note>
